What is Kendo? & Core Values

Modern Kendo training involves practitioners using a bamboo sword (竹刀) and wearing protective armor (護具), strengthening body and mind while learning respect, self-development, and courtesy (禮), faith (活人劍), and harmony of concentration, sword, and body (氣劍體一致 · 心氣力一致).
