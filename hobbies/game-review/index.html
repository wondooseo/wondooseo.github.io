Horizon Zero Dawn (PS4) · ★★★★★ Metacritic 89 · 90h (Platinum + DLC)

Guerrilla Games / SIE · Director: Mathjis de Jonge · 2017.02

"MUST play game. Horizon Zero Dawn is one of the games opened golden era of open world RPG in 2017. Hunting-like monster raid, attractive storyline based on lost technology, awesome graphics made Aloy as a powerful IP of Guerrilla Games. But there is a serious shortcoming; progression of important sub-story quests are not mandatory, and it overshadows storyline which is one of the game's strengths."

Ratchet & Clank (PS4) · ★★★★ Metacritic 85 · 31h (Platinum / NG+)

Insomniac Games / SIE · Director: Shaun McCabe / Chad Dezern / Brian Allgeier · 2016.04

"Classic! Ratchet & Clank series introduces players the way how they play the PlayStation console. You must play it after you own PS4. Strongly recommended for the PS4 beginners. Nice combination of space opera and black comedy behind charming cartoon graphic. But if you play it more intensively, you can find some small problems like slightly scanty storyline, or the weak chemistry between Ratchet and Clank."
